If you are thinking about installing electric blinds in Lisbon, the first question that inevitably arises is: **how much is this going to cost?**

The honest answer is: it depends. But in this article we will give you the real price ranges used in the Lisbon market in 2025, so that you can approach a quote with well-defined expectations.

## What influences the price of an electric blind?

The final cost of an electric blind installation depends on several factors:

- **Type and quality of the motor** — entry-level, mid-range, and high-end products carry different prices
- **Opening dimensions** — a 2m × 2m opening requires a more powerful motor than a 1m × 1m one
- **Need for new cabling** — if there is no electrical cable near the opening, a new circuit must be run
- **Type of blind** — venetian, roller, solar screen, blackout, and exterior blinds all have different mechanisms
- **Smart home compatibility** — bidirectional motors with a hub carry an additional cost

### Real-world quote example

A Lisbon flat with **4 windows** to motorise, with no existing cabling:

- 4 mid-range motors: 4 × €200 = **€800**
- Cabling for 4 openings: 4 × €60 = **€240**
- **Estimated total: €1,040 to €1,200**

With an integrated smart home system, add between €100 and €200 for the hub and configuration.

## Why do prices vary so much?

In Lisbon, there is a significant disparity in prices within the blind installation market. You will find quotes ranging from €80 per unit up to €500. What sets these proposals apart?

**Very low prices (below €120/unit)** generally indicate:
- Motors from unknown brands with no spare-parts guarantee
- Installation with no workmanship warranty
- Improvised or non-certified cabling

**Prices in the correct range (€150–€300/unit)** include:
- Quality motor with a guaranteed spare-parts supply
- Minimum 1-year warranty on both labour and motor
- Installation by technicians with certified training

## Is it worth motorising existing blinds?

Yes, provided the existing blind is in good condition. Motorising an existing manual blind typically costs between **€120 and €200 per unit**, saving on the cost of the blind itself.

However, not all blinds are compatible with retrofitted motorisation. The factors that determine feasibility are:
- The blind's axle must have a diameter of at least 40 mm
- The blind box must have space to accommodate the motor
- The winding mechanism must be in good condition
